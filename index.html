Account Deletion — Quizzar Go (by MalixCraft)

To request deletion of your Quizzar Go account and associated data:

  1. Email us at your-support-email@example.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request"
  2. Include the email address associated with your Quizzar Go account
  3. We will process your request within 7 business days

What gets deleted

  • Your account and login credentials
  • Your qToken balance and transaction history
  • Your saved quizzes, chat history, and generated content

What may be retained

  • Anonymized usage data that cannot be tied back to you, for product improvement purposes
  • Records required for legal or financial compliance (e.g. purchase records), retained as required by law

Deletion requests are processed manually and typically completed within 7 business days of your request.
